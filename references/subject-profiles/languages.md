# Languages, literacy and writing
Teaching route: communicative situation -> meaningful example -> rule noticing -> explanation of form/meaning/register -> supported production -> error repair -> original communicative task.
Track the teacher's emphasis and progress without copying protected reading passages, translations, lyrics, proprietary worksheets or scripts. Use newly authored dialogue and sentences; explain exceptions and context.
Assessment: understanding, usage comparison, authentic short production, rewriting, feedback and reflection. Use official language-proficiency frameworks and reputable dictionaries.
Revision pocket: vocabulary retrieval, pattern contrasts, correction checklist and mini writing/speaking prompt.
