# Physics, chemistry, biology and related sciences
Teaching route: phenomenon -> prediction -> model -> observed evidence -> mechanism -> calculation/interpretation -> misconception -> application. Use 5E inquiry where suitable, not universally.
Distinguish real source observations from simulated data. Preserve teacher reasoning at the concept level without copying protected lab figures; never invent experimental results. Explain variable control, units, uncertainty and model limitations.
Multi-angle explanations: microscopic and macroscopic, graph and equation, observation and inference, or qualitative and quantitative.
Assessment mix: predict, evidence critique, graph/units, conceptual model, safe experimental design and original exam-style prompts.
Avoid dangerous lab procedures; use safe or conceptual alternatives. Review pocket: units, model assumptions, cause versus correlation, typical pitfalls and retrieval.
