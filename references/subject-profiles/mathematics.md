# Mathematics
Teaching route: motivation -> definition -> intuitive model -> statement -> missing algebra steps -> formal derivation/proof -> possible wrong move -> corrected counterexample -> graduated practice.
Explain board transitions, assumptions and domain limits. State clearly whether a supposed teacher error is verified or uncertain. For difficult ideas contrast numerical intuition, graph, symbolic calculation and proof. Analogies are not proofs.
Assessment mix: explanation, calculation, proof, counterexample, mathematical modeling and original exam-style problems. Verify answers and avoid duplicating published exam wording.
External resources: official exam specifications, universities, mathematical societies and licensed OER.
Revision pocket: formula conditions, proof outline, counterexamples, mistake checklist and five-minute recall.
