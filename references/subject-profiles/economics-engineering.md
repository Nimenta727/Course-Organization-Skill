# Economics, management and engineering
Teaching route: case/problem -> assumptions -> model/design -> worked calculation -> alternatives -> failure modes -> real-world interpretation.
Economics: compare competing assumptions, evaluate causal limits, interpret authentic sourced data and explain opportunity costs and sensitivity. For current figures consult dated official statistical reports.
Engineering: units, boundaries, safety, verification, design constraints, systematic debugging and trade-offs; favor simulations where real-world experiments might be unsafe.
Assessment: calculation/model interpretation, evidence critique, decision memo, original design challenge, verification and boundary cases. Do not copy protected instructor cases.
Review pocket: assumptions, formulas/units, trade-off table, key pitfalls and original exam-style question.
