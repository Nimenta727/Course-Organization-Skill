# Teacher fidelity / 教师授课保真规范

CORE: The teacher's evidenced teaching progression leads the course; AI expands explanations rather than replaces that progression with a generic textbook. Evidence and copyright permissions are separate. Neither attendance nor access to a slide deck authorizes public copying, close paraphrasing or republication.

## Private per-element evidence ledger
For every meaningful element from authorized materials, record source ID and actually observed location (page/slide/board/time), kind, objective, concise observation, interpretation and confidence, missing steps, corresponding chapter section, response, and rights status. Kinds include concept, board-step, example, demonstration, error demo, question, emphasis, transition, diagram and assignment.

Important elements must be accounted for: explicitly represented in independently written lessons, kept private with a reason, excluded due to rights or marked unresolved. Do not silently omit teacher errors, jumps in reasoning or emphasis simply because they are inconvenient. The ledger remains private; coverage does not mean reproducing original expression.

## Teacher-characteristic preservation
- Preserve evidenced sequence, emphasis, prerequisite logic, question-to-answer progression and conceptual transitions.
- Expand skipped intermediate algebra, algorithms, assumptions, explanations and examples. Use your own prose and new examples for publicly sharable work when allowed.
- Preserve common classroom misconceptions and observed mistakes as learning topics. Never assume the teacher intended a mistake unless the material makes that explicit.
- Distinguish: OBSERVED classroom evidence, HYPOTHESIZED intention, AI-ORIGINAL enrichment, UNKNOWN / unable to assess.
- If teacher-specific expressive organization, unusual original problem, figures or wording may be protected, do not reproduce or closely transform for public release without permission. Rights review may require a newly designed independent teaching sequence while acknowledging the teacher's general topical influence.

## Error-demo protocol
1. Verify what line or representation is actually wrong. Distinguish uncertain notation from demonstrable mathematical/technical error.
2. Determine whether intent is explicitly documented; otherwise state a hypothesis with evidence and a plausible alternative.
3. Recreate the underlying misconception in a new self-contained example, not a pasted protected original.
4. Trace failure; give a corrected derivation or program; explain boundary cases and self-check.
5. Link the error to a new formative check and original exam-style trap.
Never erase an error demo silently, invent an instructor quotation, or claim intention as fact.

## Coverage and review criteria
Assess all meaningful segments, sequencing fidelity, emphases, complete intermediate reasoning, error-case treatment, factual verification and evidence honesty. Reviewers inspect the teacher map and actual draft. Mechanical counting cannot establish teaching fidelity or legal compliance.
