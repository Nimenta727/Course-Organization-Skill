# Code of Conduct / 社区行为守则

This project welcomes respectful, evidence-based collaboration among teachers, students, researchers and developers.

- Treat other contributors respectfully regardless of their background, educational experience, age, language or skill level.
- Critique ideas and reproducible behavior, not individuals. Avoid harassment, abuse, threats, discrimination, intimidation and public shaming.
- Do not publish another person's private information, student records, copyrighted teacher materials, unreleased examination questions or confidential attachments.
- Discuss pedagogy with evidence and humility; uncertainty and alternative explanations are normal, especially when interpreting instructor intent.
- For moderation concerns, use GitHub's reporting tools or open an appropriately non-sensitive issue. For vulnerabilities or private-data disclosures, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting details publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive contributions, lock discussions or restrict participation to preserve a safe and useful project. Moderation decisions should be proportionate and documented where appropriate, without exposing private information.

**中文概述：** 尊重他人，围绕事实和可复现的问题讨论，不公开教师私有材料、学生隐私或未经授权的考试资料。维护者可采取必要的管理措施。安全问题按 SECURITY.md 的非公开渠道报告。
